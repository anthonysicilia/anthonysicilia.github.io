Bio

Anthony Sicilia is an Assistant Professor of Computer Science at West Virginia University. His research combines statistical learning theory and computational linguistics to study reasoning, uncertainty, and communication in artificial intelligence systems. His work has been recognized with outstanding paper awards, including at the Conference on Uncertainty in Artificial Intelligence (UAI), and has been funded by the National Science Foundation. He received his Ph.D. in Computer Science from Northeastern University and his B.S. in Mathematics from the University of Pittsburgh.
